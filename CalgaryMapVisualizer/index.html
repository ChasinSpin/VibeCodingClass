Install Calgary Atlas

Serve this folder over localhost or HTTPS. In Chrome or Edge, choose “Install app” in the browser menu. On iPhone or iPad, open in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen.

The app and previously loaded datasets are available offline after your first visit. Background map tiles require an internet connection.
